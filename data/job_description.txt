Job Title: Junior Data Scientist
Company: Sample Tech Co.
Location: Amman, Jordan (Hybrid)

About the Role
We are looking for a Junior Data Scientist to join our analytics team. You will work with senior engineers to clean data, build machine learning models, and present insights to the business.

Responsibilities
- Collect, clean, and prepare data for analysis.
- Build and evaluate machine learning models.
- Write SQL queries to extract data from company databases.
- Present findings clearly to technical and non-technical colleagues.

Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, or a related field.
- At least 1 year of experience in data analysis or machine learning (internships and academic projects count).
- Strong programming skills in Python.
- Good knowledge of SQL.
- Experience with machine learning libraries such as scikit-learn.
- Good command of English, written and spoken.

Nice to Have
- Experience with deep learning frameworks such as TensorFlow or PyTorch.
- A cloud certification (AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud).
- Experience with data visualization tools such as Power BI or Tableau.
- Fluency in Arabic.
- Experience using Git and GitHub.
